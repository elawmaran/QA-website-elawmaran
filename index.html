Featured product · 2023 – 2026

Elite Bonusing Suite QA Lead

The Elite Bonusing Suite (EBS) is Light & Wonder's casino bonusing software. It lets a casino surprise players with bonuses, free play and rewards right on the slot machine screen, without stopping their game. To do that, it works with the casino's two main data systems, SDS and CMP, explained below.

I led QA for EBS game applications. Click any machine below to follow a bonus event through the checks it has to pass.

EBSElite Bonusing Suite

The bonus engine. It decides when a player gets a bonus or reward.

iVIEWOn-machine screen

The small screen on each slot machine where bonus messages and offers appear.

SDSSlot Data System

The casino's data system for its slot machines. It records every machine and every game played.

CMPCasino Marketplace

The casino's player data platform. It knows each loyalty-card member and their points and rewards.

In short: SDS and CMP hold the casino's data, and EBS uses that data to deliver bonuses and rewards to players through iVIEW.
